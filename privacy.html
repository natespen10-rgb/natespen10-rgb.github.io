Privacy policy

StoryCut is used only by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, StoryCut can upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and read that channel's name and analytics. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository.

What it does not do

StoryCut does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data from other people. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
